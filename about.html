My Story

Design isn't decoration it's communication stripped to its purest, most powerful form.

My journey into design began with a sketch pad and a curiosity about why certain logos felt trustworthy and others felt forgettable. That question has driven every project I've taken on since.

I'm self-taught in graphic design, driven by passion and countless hours of learning through online resources, tutorials, and real-world projects. Design has always been my creative outlet and personal pursuit. Currently, I'm pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture in my 3rd year, which might seem unrelated at first but it's shaped how I approach design. The mix of creativity and strategic thinking from agriculture carries into my design work, giving me a unique perspective on visual problem-solving.

I founded Josiah Graphics as a studio where strategy and artistry meet without compromise. Every client gets a designer who listens, a process that's transparent, and a result that exceeds expectation.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring typography history, mentoring young creatives, or chasing good light with a camera.

What I Believe

Design principles

01.

Clarity Above All

The best design communicates instantly. I strip away everything that doesn't serve the message, leaving only what earns its place on the canvas.

02.

Intent in Every Detail

Kerning, colour temperature, weight contrast nothing is arbitrary. Every micro-decision compounds into a macro impression that defines the brand.

03.

Strategy Before Style

Beautiful design that doesn't work is decoration. I start with your audience, your goals, and your competitive landscape then I make it beautiful.

04.

Long-term Thinking

Trends age. Strong visual identities don't. I design for longevity, building systems flexible enough to grow with your brand over years, not seasons.
