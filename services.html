How I Work

The design process

Every project follows a clear, collaborative process no black boxes, no surprises. You'll know exactly where we are at every step.

1

Discovery

Deep dive into your brand, audience, competitors, and goals through a structured brief session.

2

Research

Market research, mood boarding, and visual direction building the strategic foundation for design decisions.

3

Concept

Initial design explorations presented with rationale usually 2 to 3 directions for you to evaluate and feedback on.

4

Refinement

Iterating on the chosen direction with your feedback until the design is precisely right.

5

Delivery

Final files handed over in all required formats, with guidelines for consistent use going forward.
